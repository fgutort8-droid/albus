# App wording audit — 4 November 2026

Base: `48185b6`. Worktree: `~/Developer/albus-app-wording`. Branch: `app/wording-matches-behaviour`.

This is a source-to-copy audit, not certification of live services or App Store configuration. It covers the app-authored strings in all 72 Swift files under `ios/App/Albus/` plus the 3 imported `AlbusCore` notification corpus files: screens, shell, components, service failures, models, accessibility and notifications. [The inventory](string-inventory.tsv) preserves all 1978 quoted source lines, including internal literals, previews and sample data, so the scope can be reviewed. Labels, names, format strings and controls without factual promises were checked against their associated actions/values; they are not separate marketing claims. Dynamic model-generated prose and third-party pages cannot be exhaustively verified as fixed app strings.

There are 79 additional grouped claim rows below, 139 exact changed spans in [the before/after evidence table](changes.md), 115 individually listed [notification templates](notifications.md), and 225 individually listed third-party descriptions in [the reported catalogue appendix](external-tools.md). Together these form the audit table; corrected claims are not omitted just because they moved to the change ledger. Repeated variants are grouped only where their underlying implementation is shared.

**true** means supported by source under the conditions stated; **fixed** means wording changed; **reported** means unresolved or externally unverifiable, with recommended wording. A prompt instruction establishes intended AI behavior, not guaranteed output quality.
