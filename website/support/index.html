Common questions

I have a new phone and my plan is gone

Open Albus, go to Settings → Restore purchases, signed in to the App Store with the same Apple ID you subscribed with. Your subscription moves to the new phone. Restore purchases does not restore assignments or other work from your previous anonymous account.

How do I cancel?

In Albus, Settings → Manage subscription, or on your iPhone, Settings → your name → Subscriptions → Albus. You keep your plan until the end of the time you have paid for.

How do I get a refund?

Apple handles payments and refunds. Request one at reportaproblem.apple.com.

I paid, but Albus still shows Free

It can take a minute for a new plan to reach your account. Pull down on the Settings screen to refresh. If it still shows Free after a few minutes, use Restore purchases, and write to us if that doesn't fix it.

Marking or an AI plan said to try again later

Albus limits AI use to keep it affordable. When a limit is reached, plans can be made on your phone instead. Marking becomes available when the applicable limit resets; this may take longer than the same day.

Is my work private?

Photos and files selected for marking are turned into text on your phone. The original file stays there; the text is sent for marking. We save marks and feedback, which can include short excerpts from your work. The privacy policy has the details.

How do I delete my account?

In Albus, Settings → Delete account. Cancel any subscription first: deleting your account doesn't stop Apple from billing it.
